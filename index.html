About Jess

A lifetime of learning by doing.

I’m Jess Krick. I’ve never been very good at staying in one lane. I’ve worked with my hands, run equipment, built and remodeled, raised cattle, worked the land, roasted coffee, made music, worked on machines—and kept asking questions along the way.

Sometimes you just want to get away from the noise, put on an old pair of boots, work in the dirt and reconnect with what’s real. Come along. We’ll learn together.
